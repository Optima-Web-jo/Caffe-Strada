Our Story

A Taste of Italy in the Heart of Amman

Founded in 2018, Caffè Strada brings authentic Italian coffee culture to Abdoun, offering a peaceful retreat from the bustling city. Our name, meaning "street café" in Italian, reflects our commitment to creating a welcoming space where neighbors can gather, work, or simply enjoy a moment of tranquility.

We proudly serve Attibassi espresso, a premium blend roasted in Bologna since 1930, ensuring every cup carries the authentic flavors of Italian coffee tradition. Our beans are ethically sourced and freshly roasted to preserve their complex aromas.

As a completely smoke-free establishment, we provide a clean, healthy environment where you can savor the true taste of our coffee and food without compromise. Our European-inspired menu features seasonal ingredients and artisanal preparation methods.

Whether you're joining us for your morning espresso, a working lunch, or an evening dessert, we invite you to experience la dolce vita at Caffè Strada.
