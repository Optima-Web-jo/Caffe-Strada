Our Story

Caffè Strada began with a passion for the relaxed European coffee culture found along the streets of Italy. Our owners envisioned a welcoming gathering place in Abdoun where friends and colleagues could enjoy premium espresso drinks and fresh fare in a smoke-free environment.

We proudly brew Attibassi coffee beans imported from Bologna, renowned for their balanced flavor and smooth finish. Pair your cappuccino with a toasted panini, a crisp salad, or one of our artisanal desserts, all prepared with quality ingredients.

Whether you’re meeting up with friends or settling in to work, our bright and airy café is designed to help you relax and enjoy the moment.
